    Classical batch preprocessing breaks at the extremes of modern machine learning: static dataframes fit neatly in RAM, ground-truth labels are assumed clean, and missing cells follow parametric linearity. Frontier production systems reject all three assumptions [Schelter2018, Montiel2021]. Real pipelines confront non-linear manifold missingness repaired via GAIN [Yoon2018], infinite telemetry streams demanding exact \(\mathcal{O}(1)\)-memory Welford & EWMA estimators [Welford1962], systemic crowd-worker mistakes detected by Confident Learning [Northcutt2021], and strict annotation budgets steered by ActiveClean importance sampling [Krishnan2016] and HoloClean probabilistic factor graphs [Rekatsinas2017].

    2. Generative Adversarial Imputation Networks (GAIN)

    Parametric chained equations (MICE) falter when missing attributes exhibit non-linear couplings across images, clinical time series, or high-cardinality embeddings. GAIN [Yoon2018] reformulates missing-value synthesis as a minimax game directly over the observed mask geometry.

    Let \(\tilde{\mathbf{X}} \in \mathbb{R}^d\) denote the raw partially observed vector paired with binary availability mask \(\mathbf{M} \in \{0, 1\}^d\) (\(M_j = 1\) if observed, \(0\) if missing) and independent Gaussian noise \(\mathbf{Z} \sim \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{0}, \mathbf{I})\). The Generator \(G\) synthesizes a full vector and grafts observed entries deterministically:

    \[\bar{\mathbf{X}} = G\big(\tilde{\mathbf{X}}, \mathbf{M}, (\mathbf{1} - \mathbf{M}) \odot \mathbf{Z}\big), \qquad \hat{\mathbf{X}} = \mathbf{M} \odot \tilde{\mathbf{X}} + (\mathbf{1} - \mathbf{M}) \odot \bar{\mathbf{X}}\]

    Unlike standard GAN discriminators that judge an entire sample with a single scalar real/fake score, the GAIN Discriminator \(D(\hat{\mathbf{X}}, \mathbf{H}) \in [0, 1]^d\) outputs a vector of probabilities predicting which coordinates were originally real (\(M_j = 1\)) versus generator-imputed (\(M_j = 0\)). Crucially, \(D\) receives a partial Hint Matrix \(\mathbf{H}\):

    \[\mathbf{H} = \mathbf{B} \odot \mathbf{M} + 0.5\,(\mathbf{1} - \mathbf{B}), \qquad B_j \sim \text{Bernoulli}(p_{\text{hint}})\]

    When \(B_j = 1\), \(H_j\) reveals the true mask bit \(M_j \in \{0, 1\}\); when \(B_j = 0\), \(H_j = 0.5\) leaves coordinate \(j\) concealed for \(D\) to classify. The composite saddle-point optimization balances adversarial mask indistinguishability against exact reconstruction on observed coordinates:

    \[\min_G \max_D \; \mathbb{E}_{\tilde{\mathbf{X}}, \mathbf{M}, \mathbf{H}}\Big[ \mathbf{M}^{\top} \log D(\hat{\mathbf{X}}, \mathbf{H}) + (\mathbf{1} - \mathbf{M})^{\top} \log\big(\mathbf{1} - D(\hat{\mathbf{X}}, \mathbf{H})\big) \Big] + \alpha \, \mathbb{E}\Big[\|\mathbf{M} \odot (\tilde{\mathbf{X}} - \bar{\mathbf{X}})\|_2^2\Big]\]

    Mathematical Pitfall: Why Omitting the Hint Vector Causes Non-Identifiability

    Theorem 1 of Yoon et al. [Yoon2018] proves that if \(D\) receives no hint (\(\mathbf{H} \equiv \mathbf{0.5}\)), an adversarial Nash equilibrium admits infinitely many spurious conditional distributions \(G\) that fool \(D\) without matching \(P(\mathbf{X} \mid \mathbf{M})\). Conditioning \(D\) on at least \(d - 1\) revealed mask coordinates forces \(G\) to match the true conditional distribution everywhere simultaneously.

    3. Online Streaming Moments: Welford's \(\mathcal{O}(1)\) Recurrence vs. EWMA Drift

    Infinite event streams (IoT transducers, limit-order-book ticks, clickstreams) prohibit materializing historical arrays in memory. Computing variance naively via the textbook single-pass formula \(s^2 = \frac{1}{n-1}\big(\sum x_i^2 - \frac{1}{n}(\sum x_i)^2\big)\) suffers catastrophic cancellation under IEEE-754 float64 arithmetic whenever the process mean dominates dispersion (\(\mu \gg \sigma\), such as Unix epoch timestamps \(\sim 1.7 \times 10^9\) or tight financial prices): subtracting two huge near-equal numbers wipes out mantissa bits, producing negative variances.

    Welford's Algorithm [Welford1962, Montiel2021] updates centered second moments \(\mathcal{O}(1)\) time and \(\mathcal{O}(d)\) memory without squaring raw coordinates:

    \[\delta_n = x_n - \mu_{n-1}, \qquad \mu_n = \mu_{n-1} + \frac{\delta_n}{n}, \qquad M_{2, n} = M_{2, n-1} + \delta_n \, (x_n - \mu_n), \qquad s_n^2 = \frac{M_{2, n}}{n - 1}\]

    Non-Stationary Concept Drift and Exponentially Weighted Moments (EWMA)

    Unweighted Welford treats arrival \(x_1\) and arrival \(x_{100{,}000}\) equally with weight \(1/n\). When the live sensor regime undergoes concept drift (e.g., recalibration shift \(\mu: 100 \to 140\)), historical inertia freezes Welford's normalization scale for hours. Exponentially Weighted Moving Averages (EWMA) replace uniform weighting \(1/n\) with constant forgetting factor \(\alpha \in (0, 1]\) (effective window \(N_{\text{eff}} \approx \frac{2}{\alpha} - 1\)):

    \[\tilde{\mu}_t = \alpha \, x_t + (1 - \alpha) \, \tilde{\mu}_{t-1}, \qquad \tilde{s}_t^2 = (1 - \alpha)\Big(\tilde{s}_{t-1}^2 + \alpha \, (x_t - \tilde{\mu}_{t-1})^2\Big)\]

    4. Confident Learning & Programmatic Label Pruning

    Large-scale crowd-sourced or weak-supervision datasets routinely harbor \(3\%\) to \(30\%\) corrupted annotations [Northcutt2021]. Simply removing "low-confidence" samples discards valuable ambiguous boundary examples (genuine aleatoric uncertainty) alongside actual label flips. Confident Learning separates intrinsic difficulty from systematic mislabeling by estimating the joint distribution between observed noisy labels \(\tilde{y}\) and latent true labels \(y^*\) strictly from out-of-fold predicted probabilities \(\hat{p}(\tilde{y}=k \mid x)\).

    1. Per-Class Self-Confidence Threshold (\(t_j\)): Compute each class's expected out-of-fold self-confidence among examples bearing observed label \(j\):

    $\(t_j = \frac{1}{|X_{\tilde{y}=j}|} \sum_{x \in X_{\tilde{y}=j}} \hat{p}(\tilde{y}=j \mid x)\)$

    1. Confident Joint Matrix (\(\mathbf{C}_{\tilde{y}, y^*}\)): Assign item \((x, \tilde{y}=i)\) to latent cell \((i, j)\) if \(\hat{p}(\tilde{y}=j \mid x) \ge t_j\) (breaking multiple threshold crossings via \(\arg\max_j \hat{p}(\tilde{y}=j \mid x)\)). Normalizing rows against observed class marginals yields the calibrated noise transition distribution \(\hat{\mathbf{Q}}_{\tilde{y}, y^*}\).
    2. Prune-By-Noise-Rate (PBNR): Given estimated off-diagonal error count \(N_{i \to j} = \lfloor N \cdot \hat{Q}_{\tilde{y}=i, y^*=j} \rfloor\), rank observations with label \(\tilde{y}=i\) in ascending order of confidence margin \(\hat{p}(\tilde{y}=i \mid x) - \hat{p}(\tilde{y}=j \mid x)\) and prune the top \(N_{i \to j}\) worst-margin violations. Iterating one or two passes removes boundary skew induced when asymmetric noise warps initial thresholds \(t_j\).

    5. ActiveClean & HoloClean Weak Supervision Loops

    ActiveClean: Unbiased SGD Under Selective Repair

    Cleaning millions of records is financially prohibitive when human domain experts must inspect rows manually. ActiveClean [Krishnan2016] couples convex empirical risk minimization with cleaning: it prioritizes dirty batches exhibiting maximal gradient sensitivity \(q_i \propto \|\nabla_\theta \ell(x_i, y_i; \theta)\|_2\) because noisy outliers exert the largest distortion on learned model weights.

    However, training directly on non-uniformly selected clean records introduces severe sampling bias. ActiveClean preserves exact unbiasedness using the Horvitz-Thompson / Hansen-Hurwitz inverse-probability weighting theorem: drawing \(k\) records with probability \(q_i\), the weighted batch gradient satisfies:

    \[\mathbb{E}\!\left[\frac{1}{k}\sum_{m=1}^k \frac{\nabla_\theta \ell(x_{i_m}, y_{i_m}^*; \theta)}{N \, q_{i_m}}\right] = \sum_{i=1}^N q_i \cdot \frac{\nabla_\theta \ell(x_i, y_i^*; \theta)}{N \, q_i} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \nabla_\theta \ell(x_i, y_i^*; \theta) = \nabla_\theta \mathcal{L}_{\text{clean}}(\theta)\]

    HoloClean: Probabilistic Factor-Graph Repairs

    Where ActiveClean fixes training gradients interactively, HoloClean [Rekatsinas2017] treats structural attribute repair as Maximum A Posteriori (MAP) inference over a factor graph. Functional denial constraints (e.g., \(\text{ZIP} \to \text{State}\)), external knowledge bases, and co-occurrence statistics become weighted potential functions \(\phi_m(c)\) over candidate repair values \(c \in \mathcal{D}\):

    \[P(C = c) = \frac{1}{Z} \exp\!\left( \sum_{m} w_m \, \phi_m(c) \right)\]

    7. Production Implementation: Welford/EWMA Streamer, Confident Joint Pruner & ActiveClean Verifier

    The following self-contained Python suite uses strict Pydantic V2 verification schemas to assert: (1) numerical exactness of \(\mathcal{O}(1)\) Welford moments vs. full batch computation alongside EWMA post-shift adaptation, (2) label-noise precision/recall and downstream out-of-sample test accuracy lift from iterative Confident Joint PBNR pruning, and (3) unbiasedness of ActiveClean Horvitz-Thompson importance weighting compared to unweighted selective sampling.

    """
Verify Welford O(1) vs EWMA Concept Drift, Confident Learning Joint Matrix Pruner,
and ActiveClean Horvitz-Thompson Unbiased Gradient Estimator under Pydantic V2.
"""

from typing import Tuple
import numpy as np
from pydantic import BaseModel, ConfigDict, Field
from sklearn.linear_model import LogisticRegression
from sklearn.model_selection import StratifiedKFold


class StreamingMomentsReport(BaseModel):
    model_config = ConfigDict(strict=True)
    samples_processed: int = Field(gt=1)
    welford_mean: float
    batch_mean: float
    mean_abs_error: float = Field(lt=1e-9)
    welford_std: float
    batch_std: float
    std_abs_error: float = Field(lt=1e-9)
    ewma_post_shift_mean: float
    welford_post_shift_mean: float


class ConfidentLearningReport(BaseModel):
    model_config = ConfigDict(strict=True)
    n_train_samples: int = Field(gt=0)
    injected_errors: int = Field(gt=0)
    flagged_errors: int = Field(gt=0)
    precision: float = Field(ge=0.7, le=1.0)
    recall: float = Field(ge=0.7, le=1.0)
    noisy_test_accuracy: float = Field(ge=0.0, le=1.0)
    pruned_test_accuracy: float = Field(ge=0.0, le=1.0)
    accuracy_lift: float = Field(gt=0.02)


class ActiveCleanHTReport(BaseModel):
    model_config = ConfigDict(strict=True)
    n_monte_carlo_trials: int = Field(ge=100)
    ht_relative_bias: float = Field(lt=0.05)
    unweighted_relative_bias: float = Field(gt=0.10)


class WelfordEWMAStream:
    """Simultaneously tracks exact O(1) cumulative Welford moments and EWMA reactive mean."""

    def __init__(self, alpha: float = 0.05) -> None:
        self.count = 0
        self.mean = 0.0
        self.M2 = 0.0
        self.alpha = alpha
        self.ewma_mean = 0.0

    def update(self, x: float) -> Tuple[float, float, float]:
        self.count += 1
        delta = x - self.mean
        self.mean += delta / self.count
        delta2 = x - self.mean
        self.M2 += delta * delta2

        if self.count == 1:
            self.ewma_mean = x
        else:
            self.ewma_mean = self.alpha * x + (1.0 - self.alpha) * self.ewma_mean

        std = float(np.sqrt(self.M2 / (self.count - 1))) if self.count >= 2 else 0.0
        return self.mean, std, self.ewma_mean


class ConfidentLearningAuditor:
    """Confident Joint matrix estimator and PBNR pruner (Northcutt et al., 2021)."""

    def __init__(self, cv: int = 5) -> None:
        self.cv = cv
        self.thresholds_: np.ndarray | None = None
        self.confident_joint_: np.ndarray | None = None
        self.joint_dist_: np.ndarray | None = None

    def fit_detect(self, X: np.ndarray, y_noisy: np.ndarray, rounds: int = 2) -> np.ndarray:
        n = len(y_noisy)
        classes = np.unique(y_noisy)
        n_classes = len(classes)
        active_mask = np.ones(n, dtype=bool)
        all_flagged: list[int] = []

        for _ in range(rounds):
            active_idx = np.where(active_mask)[0]
            X_act, y_act = X[active_idx], y_noisy[active_idx]
            pred_probs = np.zeros((len(y_act), n_classes), dtype=float)

            skf = StratifiedKFold(n_splits=self.cv, shuffle=True, random_state=42)
            for train_idx, val_idx in skf.split(X_act, y_act):
                clf = LogisticRegression(solver="lbfgs", C=1.0, max_iter=1000)
                clf.fit(X_act[train_idx], y_act[train_idx])
                pred_probs[val_idx] = clf.predict_proba(X_act[val_idx])

            # 1. Class self-confidence thresholds t_j = E[p(y=j | x) | y_tilde = j]
            self.thresholds_ = np.array(
                [
                    np.mean(pred_probs[y_act == j, j]) if np.any(y_act == j) else 1.0 / n_classes
                    for j in range(n_classes)
                ],
                dtype=float,
            )

            # 2. Construct Uncalibrated Confident Joint C[y_tilde, y*]
            joint = np.zeros((n_classes, n_classes), dtype=int)
            for i in range(len(y_act)):
                qualifying = [k for k in range(n_classes) if pred_probs[i, k] >= self.thresholds_[k]]
                if qualifying:
                    y_star = max(qualifying, key=lambda k: pred_probs[i, k])
                    joint[int(y_act[i]), y_star] += 1
            self.confident_joint_ = joint

            # 3. Calibrate to Joint Distribution preserving observed marginals
            calibrated = np.zeros((n_classes, n_classes), dtype=float)
            for i_cls in range(n_classes):
                row_sum = joint[i_cls].sum()
                obs_count = int(np.sum(y_act == i_cls))
                if row_sum > 0:
                    calibrated[i_cls] = joint[i_cls] * (obs_count / row_sum)
                else:
                    calibrated[i_cls, i_cls] = float(obs_count)
            self.joint_dist_ = calibrated / np.sum(calibrated)

            # 4. Prune-By-Noise-Rate (PBNR): prune top off-diagonal margin violations
            round_flagged: list[int] = []
            for i_cls in range(n_classes):
                obs_local = np.where(y_act == i_cls)[0]
                for j_cls in range(n_classes):
                    if i_cls == j_cls:
                        continue
                    est_error_count = int(round(calibrated[i_cls, j_cls]))
                    if est_error_count > 0 and len(obs_local) > 0:
                        margins = pred_probs[obs_local, i_cls] - pred_probs[obs_local, j_cls]
                        worst_local = obs_local[np.argsort(margins)[:est_error_count]]
                        round_flagged.extend(active_idx[worst_local].tolist())

            if not round_flagged:
                break
            all_flagged.extend(round_flagged)
            active_mask[round_flagged] = False

        return np.array(sorted(set(all_flagged)), dtype=int)


def run_all_verifications() -> None:
    rng = np.random.default_rng(42)

    # 1. Exact Welford Equivalence + Concept Drift Check
    pre_shift = rng.normal(loc=100.0, scale=15.0, size=5000)
    post_shift = rng.normal(loc=140.0, scale=15.0, size=500)
    full_stream = np.concatenate([pre_shift, post_shift])

    tracker = WelfordEWMAStream(alpha=0.05)
    for val in full_stream:
        w_mean, w_std, ewma_m = tracker.update(float(val))

    b_mean = float(np.mean(full_stream))
    b_std = float(np.std(full_stream, ddof=1))

    stream_report = StreamingMomentsReport(
        samples_processed=tracker.count,
        welford_mean=w_mean,
        batch_mean=b_mean,
        mean_abs_error=abs(w_mean - b_mean),
        welford_std=w_std,
        batch_std=b_std,
        std_abs_error=abs(w_std - b_std),
        ewma_post_shift_mean=round(ewma_m, 3),
        welford_post_shift_mean=round(w_mean, 3),
    )
    assert abs(ewma_m - 140.0) < 3.0, f"EWMA should track shifted mean ~140, got {ewma_m}"
    assert abs(w_mean - 140.0) > 25.0, "Unweighted Welford retains pre-shift historical inertia"

    # 2. Confident Learning Label Pruner on Asymmetric Noisy Decision Boundary
    n_train, n_test = 1500, 1000
    n_pos = (n_train + n_test) // 2
    n_neg = (n_train + n_test) - n_pos
    X_pos = rng.normal(loc=[1.6, 1.6, 0.0, 0.0], scale=0.85, size=(n_pos, 4))
    X_neg = rng.normal(loc=[-1.6, -1.6, 0.0, 0.0], scale=0.85, size=(n_neg, 4))
    X_all = np.vstack([X_pos, X_neg])
    y_true_all = np.concatenate([np.ones(n_pos, dtype=int), np.zeros(n_neg, dtype=int)])
    perm = rng.permutation(n_train + n_test)
    X_all, y_true_all = X_all[perm], y_true_all[perm]

    X_train, y_train_clean = X_all[:n_train], y_true_all[:n_train]
    X_test, y_test_clean = X_all[n_train:], y_true_all[n_train:]

    # Inject 30% asymmetric label flips (class 1 mislabeled as class 0)
    pos_idx = np.where(y_train_clean == 1)[0]
    noisy_indices = rng.choice(pos_idx, size=int(0.30 * len(pos_idx)), replace=False)
    y_train_noisy = y_train_clean.copy()
    y_train_noisy[noisy_indices] = 0

    auditor = ConfidentLearningAuditor(cv=5)
    flagged = auditor.fit_detect(X_train, y_train_noisy, rounds=2)
    true_positives = len(set(flagged).intersection(set(noisy_indices)))
    precision = true_positives / len(flagged)
    recall = true_positives / len(noisy_indices)

    clf_noisy = LogisticRegression(solver="lbfgs").fit(X_train, y_train_noisy)
    acc_noisy = float(clf_noisy.score(X_test, y_test_clean))

    keep_mask = np.ones(n_train, dtype=bool)
    keep_mask[flagged] = False
    clf_pruned = LogisticRegression(solver="lbfgs").fit(X_train[keep_mask], y_train_noisy[keep_mask])
    acc_pruned = float(clf_pruned.score(X_test, y_test_clean))

    cl_report = ConfidentLearningReport(
        n_train_samples=n_train,
        injected_errors=len(noisy_indices),
        flagged_errors=len(flagged),
        precision=round(precision, 4),
        recall=round(recall, 4),
        noisy_test_accuracy=round(acc_noisy, 4),
        pruned_test_accuracy=round(acc_pruned, 4),
        accuracy_lift=round(acc_pruned - acc_noisy, 4),
    )

    # 3. ActiveClean Horvitz-Thompson Importance Sampling Verification
    n_pop = 200
    gradients = np.linspace(0.5, 10.0, n_pop)
    true_mean_grad = float(np.mean(gradients))
    probs = gradients / np.sum(gradients)
    batch_k = 30
    n_trials = 2000

    ht_estimates, naive_estimates = [], []
    for _ in range(n_trials):
        idx = rng.choice(n_pop, size=batch_k, replace=True, p=probs)
        q_i = probs[idx]
        ht_estimates.append(np.mean(gradients[idx] / (n_pop * q_i)))
        naive_estimates.append(np.mean(gradients[idx]))

    ht_rel_bias = abs(float(np.mean(ht_estimates)) - true_mean_grad) / true_mean_grad
    naive_rel_bias = abs(float(np.mean(naive_estimates)) - true_mean_grad) / true_mean_grad

    ac_report = ActiveCleanHTReport(
        n_monte_carlo_trials=n_trials,
        ht_relative_bias=round(ht_rel_bias, 5),
        unweighted_relative_bias=round(naive_rel_bias, 5),
    )

    print("=== Production Frontier Preprocessing Verification ===")
    print(f"1. Welford O(1) vs Batch  -> Mean Abs Error: {stream_report.mean_abs_error:.2e} | Std Abs Error: {stream_report.std_abs_error:.2e}")
    print(f"   Post-Shift (+40 Drift) -> EWMA: {stream_report.ewma_post_shift_mean} vs. Lagging Welford: {stream_report.welford_post_shift_mean}")
    print(f"2. Confident Learning     -> Prec: {cl_report.precision:.4f} | Rec: {cl_report.recall:.4f} | Clean Acc: {cl_report.noisy_test_accuracy:.4f} -> {cl_report.pruned_test_accuracy:.4f} (+{cl_report.accuracy_lift:.4f})")
    print(f"3. ActiveClean HT Bias    -> HT Rel Bias: {ac_report.ht_relative_bias:.5f} vs. Unweighted Bias: {ac_report.unweighted_relative_bias:.5f}")
    print("✓ All Pydantic V2 frontier assertions passed cleanly!")


if __name__ == "__main__":
    run_all_verifications()

    9. Scenario-Based Diagnostic Micro-Checks

    Micro-Check 1: Why GAIN Requires Partial Mask Hints (\(\mathbf{H}\))

    Suppose a generative data engineer removes the Hint Matrix \(\mathbf{H}\) from GAIN and feeds only the completed matrix \(\hat{\mathbf{X}}\) into Discriminator \(D(\hat{\mathbf{X}})\). Why does training fail to converge uniquely toward the true missing-data distribution?

    Reveal Detailed Solution

    Without \(\mathbf{H}\), infinitely many structurally incorrect generators achieve zero adversarial loss against \(D\).

    1. By Yoon et al. [Yoon2018], \(D\) attempts to discriminate binary mask patterns \(\mathbf{M}\) solely from synthesized coordinates \(\hat{\mathbf{X}}\).
    2. When \(\mathbf{H}\) is absent, multiple spurious conditional distributions \(G_1, G_2\) can share the same composite unconditional density, preventing \(D\) from penalizing conditional violations conditioned on specific subsets of observed features.
    3. Revealing all mask bits except one via \(\mathbf{H}\) isolates \(D\)'s classification test cleanly onto individual target coordinates, uniquely identifying \(P(\mathbf{X} \mid \mathbf{M})\).
    Micro-Check 2: Catastrophic Cancellation on Epoch Timestamps & Welford vs. EWMA

    A telemetry engineer computes running variance over microsecond Unix timestamps (\(x_t \approx 1.73 \times 10^{9} \pm 0.05\)) using the single-pass formula \(\big(\sum x_t^2 - \frac{1}{n}(\sum x_t)^2\big)/(n-1)\) in IEEE-754 float64, producing negative variances (-1.4e6). Then, after switching to Welford's recurrence, an equipment calibration shifts \(\mu\) by \(+40\) units—yet the model stays miscalibrated for days. Diagnose both bugs.

    Reveal Detailed Solution

    The first bug is IEEE-754 catastrophic cancellation; the second is unbounded stationary memory inertia.

    • Squaring \(1.73 \times 10^9\) yields \(\approx 3.0 \times 10^{18}\), exceeding the 53-bit significand precision (\(\approx 10^{15}\)) relative to \(\sigma^2 = 0.0025\); subtracting \((\sum x)^2/n\) discards all variance signal into floating-point rounding noise. Welford avoids this completely by subtracting the running mean before squaring: \(\delta_n = x_n - \mu_{n-1}\).
    • Once switched to plain Welford across \(10^7\) historical samples, a new point arrives with weight \(1 / 10^7\). Under non-stationary concept drift, EWMA (\(\alpha \in (0, 1]\)) decays pre-shift history exponentially with effective window \(2/\alpha - 1\).
    Micro-Check 3: ActiveClean Importance Weights Under Non-Uniform Sampling

    To repair corrupted rows cheaply, ActiveClean samples dirty records preferentially using gradient magnitude \(q_i \propto \|\nabla_\theta \ell(x_i, y_i)\|_2\). Why must each cleaned gradient step be scaled by \(w_i = 1 / (N \, q_i)\), and what happens if \(w_i\) is omitted?

    Reveal Detailed Solution

    Omitting Horvitz-Thompson weights converts active sampling into systematic objective distortion.

    By inverse-probability weighting [Krishnan2016]: $\(\mathbb{E}\!\left[\frac{1}{k}\sum_{m=1}^k \frac{\nabla \ell_{i_m}}{N \, q_{i_m}}\right] = \sum_{i=1}^N q_i \frac{\nabla \ell_i}{N \, q_i} = \nabla \mathcal{L}_{\text{clean}}(\theta)\)$ Without dividing by \(N q_i\), high-gradient outlier neighborhoods over-dominate SGD steps (27.8% gradient bias in Section 7 verification), driving \(\theta\) away from the true population ERM optimum \(\theta^*\).

    Micro-Check 4: HoloClean Factor Graphs vs. Isolated Row Rules

    How does HoloClean [Rekatsinas2017] unify relational integrity rules (e.g., \(\text{City} + \text{ZIP} \to \text{State}\)) with statistical correlations into automated table repairs?

    Reveal Detailed Solution

    HoloClean casts multi-signal holistic repair as MAP inference over a probabilistic graphical factor model.

    Rather than executing brittle priority-ordered if/else clean rules sequentially, denial constraints, external reference lookups, and empirical value co-occurrence frequencies compile into weighted feature factors \(\phi_m(c)\). Learning weights \(w_m\) via marginal maximum likelihood yields the global joint repair maximizing \(\exp\big(\sum_m w_m \phi_m(c)\big) / Z\) while respecting relational consistency.
