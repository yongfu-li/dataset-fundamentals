    Systematic 13-Track Mastery Program

    🎓 13 Comprehensive Learning Tracks • 131 Narrated Clips

    Systematic Curriculum Tracks

    Follow the complete 13-track curriculum from raw data acquisition to scalable, ethical release. Each track features guided lecture parts, bridge explanations, self-assessment quizzes, and downloadable presentation decks.

    🗺️ Track Directory

    Track 1: Introduction to Datasets (7 Parts)

    • Scope: Defines records, attributes, and data structures (structured, semi-structured, unstructured). Evaluates quality across accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness, and relevance.
    • Learning Path: 🎬 Launch Guided Chapter 1 Path & Video Clips
    • Downloads: Chapter 1 PPTX · Chapter 1 PDF
    • Companion Tools: Schema & Format Translator, Train / Val / Test Splitter.

    Track 2: Fundamentals of Data Collection (9 Parts)

    • Scope: Sampling theory and mathematical error limits. Contrasts simple random sampling, stratified sampling, and cluster sampling. Covers sample size calculation and statistical power.
    • Learning Path: 🎬 Launch Guided Chapter 2 Path & Video Clips
    • Downloads: Chapter 2 PPTX · Chapter 2 PDF
    • Companion Tools: Sampling Techniques Visualizer.

    Track 3: Ethics and Privacy in Data Work (11 Parts)

    • Scope: Governance frameworks (GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA). Mathematical privacy models including \(k\)-anonymity, \(l\)-diversity, \(t\)-closeness, and differential privacy (\(\epsilon, \delta\)).
    • Learning Path: 🎬 Launch Guided Chapter 3 Path & Video Clips
    • Downloads: Chapter 3 PPTX · Chapter 3 PDF
    • Companion Tools: Consent & PII Scrubber, De-identification Risk Checker, Ethical Decision Tree.

    Track 4: Data Annotation & Agreement (10 Parts)

    • Scope: Human-in-the-loop annotation workflows, bounding boxes, polygon segmentation, NER, and sentiment tagging. Inter-Annotator Agreement (IAA) calculation: Cohen's \(\kappa\), Fleiss' \(\kappa\), and Krippendorff's \(\alpha\).
    • Learning Path: 🎬 Launch Guided Chapter 4 Path & Video Clips
    • Downloads: Chapter 4 PPTX · Chapter 4 PDF
    • Companion Tools: Image Annotation Lab, Text Annotation Lab, IAA Calculator.

    Track 5: Data Cleaning & Preprocessing (12 Parts)

    • Scope: Identifying missing data mechanisms (MCAR, MAR, MNAR) and imputation strategies. Statistical outlier detection (Z-scores, Tukey IQR). Feature scaling (Min-Max, Standardization) and class imbalance handling (SMOTE, NearMiss).
    • Learning Path: 🎬 Launch Guided Chapter 5 Path & Video Clips
    • Downloads: Chapter 5 PPTX · Chapter 5 PDF
    • Companion Tools: Cleaning Workbench, Scaling / Encoding Lab, Class Imbalance Explorer.

    Track 6: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) (12 Parts)

    • Scope: Univariate distributions, bivariate correlations, and multivariate covariance matrices. Pearson vs. Spearman rank correlation. Identifying confounding variables and Simpson's Paradox.
    • Learning Path: 🎬 Launch Guided Chapter 6 Path & Video Clips
    • Downloads: Chapter 6 PPTX · Chapter 6 PDF
    • Companion Tools: Automated EDA Dashboard.

    Track 7: Dataset Bias and Fairness (10 Parts)

    • Scope: Taxonomy of bias (representation, historical, measurement, aggregation). Mathematical fairness definitions: Disparate Impact Ratio, Equalized Odds, Demographic Parity, and Predictive Parity.
    • Learning Path: 🎬 Launch Guided Chapter 7 Path & Video Clips
    • Downloads: Chapter 7 PPTX · Chapter 7 PDF
    • Companion Tools: Representation Bias Visualizer, Algorithmic Fairness Meter.

    Track 8: Dataset Documentation & Version Control (10 Parts)

    • Scope: Writing Gebru et al. Datasheets for Datasets. Schema validation and semantic versioning. Data Version Control (DVC) for reproducible ML pipelines.
    • Learning Path: 🎬 Launch Guided Chapter 8 Path & Video Clips
    • Downloads: Chapter 8 PPTX · Chapter 8 PDF
    • Companion Tools: Datasheet Builder, Metadata Completeness Checker, Version Timeline.

    Track 9: Advanced Data Collection Techniques (10 Parts)

    • Scope: Ethical web crawling and DOM parsing, Robots.txt compliance, API authentication, sliding-window rate limits, and real-time sensor streams.
    • Learning Path: 🎬 Launch Guided Chapter 9 Path & Video Clips
    • Downloads: Chapter 9 PPTX · Chapter 9 PDF

    Track 10: Synthetic Data Generation (10 Parts)

    • Scope: Rule-based media augmentation, back-translation, text paraphrasing, Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), and Diffusion models for tabular and image synthesis.
    • Learning Path: 🎬 Launch Guided Chapter 10 Path & Video Clips
    • Downloads: Chapter 10 PPTX · Chapter 10 PDF
    • Companion Tools: Text Augmentation Lab, Image Augmentation Lab, Media Augmentation Lab.

    Track 11: Advanced Annotation Techniques (10 Parts)

    • Scope: Active learning query strategies (uncertainty sampling, margin sampling, query-by-committee), weak supervision using programmatic labeling functions (Snorkel), and label aggregation under noisy workers.
    • Learning Path: 🎬 Launch Guided Chapter 11 Path & Video Clips
    • Downloads: Chapter 11 PPTX · Chapter 11 PDF

    Track 12: Scalable Data Management (10 Parts)

    • Scope: Distributed data architectures and the CAP theorem. Column-oriented vs. row-oriented storage (Parquet, Arrow, ORC vs. CSV). Cloud object storage (S3) and data partitioning strategies.
    • Learning Path: 🎬 Launch Guided Chapter 12 Path & Video Clips
    • Downloads: Chapter 12 PPTX · Chapter 12 PDF
    • Companion Tools: Storage Format Chooser.

    Track 13: Reproducibility and Open Science (10 Parts)

    • Scope: The FAIR data principles (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable). Open science licensing (Creative Commons CC BY / CC0, Open Data Commons ODC-By). Persistent identifiers (DOIs) and data repositories.
    • Learning Path: 🎬 Launch Guided Chapter 13 Path & Video Clips
    • Downloads: Chapter 13 PPTX · Chapter 13 PDF
    • Companion Tools: Open License Chooser.
