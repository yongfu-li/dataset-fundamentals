    📚 All 13 Curriculum Tracks

    Track 1 • Aligned with Chapter 1
    Introduction to Datasets
    Taxonomy of structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data. Core quality dimensions: accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness, and relevance.
    ⚡ 7 Guided Parts • Interactive AI Slide Decks • Schema Translator & Splitter
    Explore Track 1 →
    Track 2 • Aligned with Chapter 2
    Fundamentals of Data Collection
    Sampling theory and mathematical error limits. Simple random, stratified, and cluster sampling. Power analysis and real-world population coverage.
    ⚡ 9 Guided Parts • Interactive AI Slide Decks • Sampling Techniques Visualizer
    Explore Track 2 →
    Track 3 • Aligned with Chapter 3
    Ethics and Privacy in Data Work
    Regulatory governance under GDPR, HIPAA, and CCPA. Mathematical anonymity models: $k$-anonymity, $l$-diversity, $t$-closeness, and differential privacy ($\epsilon, \delta$).
    ⚡ 11 Guided Parts • Interactive AI Slide Decks • PII Scrubber & De-ID Risk
    Explore Track 3 →
    Track 4 • Aligned with Chapter 4
    Data Annotation & Agreement
    Human-in-the-loop annotation workflows, bounding boxes, segmentation, and NER. Inter-Annotator Agreement (IAA): Cohen's $\kappa$, Fleiss' $\kappa$, and Krippendorff's $\alpha$.
    ⚡ 10 Guided Parts • Interactive AI Slide Decks • Text/Image Lab & IAA Calculator
    Explore Track 4 →
    Track 5 • Aligned with Chapter 5
    Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
    Missing data mechanisms (MCAR, MAR, MNAR) and imputation. Statistical outlier detection (Z-scores, Tukey IQR). Feature scaling and class imbalance (SMOTE).
    ⚡ 12 Guided Parts • Interactive AI Slide Decks • Cleaning Workbench & Scaling Lab
    Explore Track 5 →
    Track 6 • Aligned with Chapter 6
    Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
    Univariate, bivariate, and multivariate inspection. Covariance structures, Pearson/Spearman correlation matrices, confounder detection, and Simpson's Paradox.
    ⚡ 12 Guided Parts • Interactive AI Slide Decks • Automated EDA Dashboard
    Explore Track 6 →
    Track 7 • Aligned with Chapter 7
    Dataset Bias and Fairness
    Representational, historical, and measurement bias. Mathematical fairness criteria: Disparate Impact Ratio, Equalized Odds, and Demographic Parity frontiers.
    ⚡ 10 Guided Parts • Interactive AI Slide Decks • Representation Visualizer & Fairness Meter
    Explore Track 7 →
    Track 8 • Aligned with Chapter 8
    Documentation & Version Control
    Standardized metadata schemas and Gebru et al. Datasheets for Datasets. Dataset version control using Git LFS and Data Version Control (DVC).
    ⚡ 10 Guided Parts • Interactive AI Slide Decks • Datasheets Builder & Version Timeline
    Explore Track 8 →
    Track 9 • Aligned with Chapter 9
    Advanced Data Collection
    Crowdsourcing consensus (Dawid-Skene EM), cyber-physical IoT & edge pipelines, Kafka event-time watermarks, and ACID data lakehouses.
    ⚡ 10 Guided Parts • Interactive AI Slide Decks • Queueing, Consensus, Edge & Lakehouse Labs
    Explore Track 9 →
    Track 10 • Aligned with Chapter 10
    Synthetic Data Generation
    Data augmentation heuristics, text paraphrasing, Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), Diffusion models, and privacy-preserving synthetic record generation.
    ⚡ 10 Guided Parts • Interactive AI Slide Decks • Text & Image Augmentation Labs
    Explore Track 10 →
    Track 11 • Aligned with Chapter 11
    Advanced Annotation Techniques
    Active learning query heuristics (uncertainty sampling, query-by-committee), weak supervision (Snorkel labeling functions), and label aggregation under noisy annotators.
    ⚡ 10 Guided Parts • Interactive AI Slide Decks • Active Learning & Snorkel Simulator
    Explore Track 11 →
    Track 12 • Aligned with Chapter 12
    Scalable Data Management
    The CAP theorem in distributed storage. Columnar formats (Apache Parquet, Arrow, ORC) vs. row stores. Data lakes, partitioning, and cloud object stores (S3).
    ⚡ 10 Guided Parts • Interactive AI Slide Decks • Storage Chooser & Parquet Lab
    Explore Track 12 →
    Track 13 • Synthesis & Governance
    Reproducibility & Open Science
    The FAIR data principles (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable). Open science licensing (Creative Commons, Open Data Commons), and persistent data identifiers (DOIs).
    ⚡ 10 Guided Parts • Interactive AI Slide Decks • Open Science Checklist & License Lab
    Explore Capstone Track 13 →
