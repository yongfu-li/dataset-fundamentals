What you will learn

  • Formalize datasets as empirical samples drawn from joint probability distributions under Data-Centric AI
  • Classify datasets across structural schemas (1NF relations, trees, manifolds) and temporal streaming dynamics
  • Analyze physical serialization layouts (row vs columnar stores, predicate pushdown, RLE/dictionary encoding)
  • Audit multi-dimensional quality frameworks, semantic defect typologies, and missingness mechanisms (MCAR/MNAR)
  • Execute four-tier exploratory data profiling across moments, Tukey IQR fences, and Simpson's paradox
  • Implement empirical risk minimization protocols, prevent data leakage, and optimize asymmetric economic thresholds
  • Govern dataset lifecycles through content-addressable versioning, RBAC masking, and cryptographic erasure
