## Objectives

- Define the five core quality dimensions: accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness, and relevance
- Diagnose semantic accuracy defects and categorize missingness mechanisms into MCAR, MAR, and MNAR
- Examine metadata architectures adhering to the FAIR data principles
- Audit dataset health scores using our in-browser diagnostic inspector
